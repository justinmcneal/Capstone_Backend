## Rating Rules

For each applicable task, record one outcome:

- **I — Independent success:** Completed correctly without help.
- **A — Assisted success:** Completed after a hint or researcher assistance.
- **U — Unsuccessful:** Could not complete or completed incorrectly.
- **B — Blocked by system:** A defect, unavailable service, or environment problem prevented completion.
- **N/A — Not included:** Task was outside the approved session.

Record time only if all participants are timed consistently. Record factual behavior rather than guessing what the participant felt.
