## Interviewer Instructions

- Obtain informed consent before beginning.
- Use the assigned respondent code; do not place a name on this guide.
- Ask the questions in Filipino, English, or the participant's preferred approved language.
- Use probes only when clarification is needed. Do not suggest a preferred answer.
- Ask permission separately before audio recording. If permission is not given, take written notes.
- Do not ask for passwords, OTPs, account numbers, private keys, exact balances, or copies of real documents.
- Remind the participant that the prototype does not guarantee a loan or influence a real lender's decision.

## Interview Opening

“Salamat sa pagsali. Nais naming malaman ang inyong karanasan at pangangailangan tungkol sa pag-unawa at paghahanda para sa loan. Walang tama o maling sagot. Maaari ninyong laktawan ang anumang tanong o ihinto ang interview anumang oras. Ang MSME Pathways ay isang academic prototype at hindi nagbibigay ng garantisadong loan approval.”

“Thank you for participating. We want to understand your experiences and needs concerning loan understanding and preparation. There are no right or wrong answers. You may skip a question or stop at any time. MSME Pathways is an academic prototype and does not guarantee loan approval.”
