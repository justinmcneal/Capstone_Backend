## Part A. Loan-Understanding Check

Select one answer for each item. One point is awarded for each correct answer. Do not show the answer key to participants.

1. **What does the total repayment amount mean? / Ano ang ibig sabihin ng kabuuang halaga ng babayaran?**

   - [ ] A. The amount originally borrowed only
   - [ ] B. The amount borrowed plus applicable interest and disclosed charges
   - [ ] C. The borrower's monthly income
   - [ ] D. The value of the borrower's business

2. **When comparing two loan offers, which information is most important to review together? / Sa paghahambing ng dalawang loan offer, alin ang dapat suriin nang magkakasama?**

   - [ ] A. App color and logo
   - [ ] B. Loan amount only
   - [ ] C. Total repayment, interest/rate information, charges, term, and due dates
   - [ ] D. Advertisement popularity

3. **What should a borrower do when a repayment term or charge is unclear? / Ano ang dapat gawin kapag hindi malinaw ang kondisyon o singil?**

   - [ ] A. Accept immediately
   - [ ] B. Ask the lender for a clear explanation before agreeing
   - [ ] C. Ignore the unclear term
   - [ ] D. Share an OTP to obtain more information

4. **What is a possible result of paying after the due date? / Ano ang posibleng mangyari kapag lumampas sa due date ang bayad?**

   - [ ] A. Applicable penalties or other consequences stated in the agreement
   - [ ] B. Automatic cancellation of all debt in every case
   - [ ] C. Guaranteed approval of another loan
   - [ ] D. No effect under any loan agreement

5. **Which information should never be shared with a chatbot, researcher, or person claiming to “guarantee” approval?**

   - [ ] A. Preferred language
   - [ ] B. General type of business
   - [ ] C. Passwords, OTPs, backup codes, or wallet private keys
   - [ ] D. General feedback about the interface

6. **What does a positive pre-qualification or readiness result mean in this prototype?**

   - [ ] A. A real lender is legally required to approve the loan
   - [ ] B. The user appears to meet the prototype's preliminary criteria, but official review is still required
   - [ ] C. The loan has already been disbursed
   - [ ] D. Repayment is optional

7. **Before submitting a formal application, which action best supports readiness?**

   - [ ] A. Check the required information and documents for completeness and accuracy
   - [ ] B. Create false business records
   - [ ] C. Hide existing obligations when asked lawfully
   - [ ] D. Share an account password with the evaluator

8. **Which statement about an AI financial-guidance chatbot is correct?**

   - [ ] A. It can guarantee approval
   - [ ] B. It replaces all lender review and professional advice
   - [ ] C. It can provide educational guidance, but its output must be checked and is not an official lending decision
   - [ ] D. It should be trusted with passwords and OTPs
